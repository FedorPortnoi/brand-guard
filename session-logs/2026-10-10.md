## 5. Three layers from one link

Requested: (1) paste any link and get legit or not, (2) similar products and listings using existing tools,
(3) manufacturers, without losing the main idea (brand protection). Decisions: for anyone (shoppers, brand owners,
any social link); manufacturers both ways, the user's choice.

Another session or tool had already built a URL-first flow in this folder (`app/listing.py`, the quick check page,
the evidence lab, upload ingest for PDF / ZIP / Office). It was improved rather than duplicated.

- Layer 1: brand settings (own site / reseller); social posts read with yt-dlp; off-platform payment flagged;
  no domain age for social or marketplace pages; brand-protection registrars and own networks as good signs
  (adidas.com and allbirds.com now green); lookalike addresses flagged; short links followed; whole-word wording,
  weak words only in titles (a YouTube description had been flagged for "copy").
- Layer 2: Brave instead of the failing DuckDuckGo; retailer pages kept by URL pattern; product photo with an
  "Open in Google Lens" link (checked: opens Lens with the photo); Lens results in the app once a SerpApi key is
  set; "Scan the web for copies of this photo".
- Layer 3: "Who makes it" and "Where copies are sourced" (Alibaba, 1688, Made-in-China, DHgate, Global Sources,
  IndiaMART), searched without the brand and filtered by the product's distinctive words.
- Live on the Allbirds Tree Runner: green; similar listings at Allbirds, Dick's, Amazon and eBay; the source search
  found a DHgate "TREEPERI" Tree Runner lookalike.
