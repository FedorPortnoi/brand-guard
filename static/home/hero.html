Before you upload

Only share a file that you own or have permission to use. Brand Guard will read it to answer your request.

  • Remove passwords and full payment-card numbers.
  • Do not upload government IDs, medical records, or private conversations without permission.
  • Relevant text or images may be checked with configured research providers.
