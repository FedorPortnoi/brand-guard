Privacy notice

This notice describes Brand Guard's current prototype service. It applies to the web checker, the Evidence Lab, and files or messages you submit for analysis.

Information we process

  • Text, links, store names, account names, and optional screenshots, videos, PDFs, or receipts that you choose to submit.
  • Information inferred from a submission, such as domains, seller names, page text, threat signals, search results, and public registration records.
  • Basic technical request information normally handled by the web server, such as IP address, browser information, timestamps, and error details.

Why we process it

We use this information to answer your request, find observable fraud or legitimacy signals, provide supporting sources, prevent abuse, and diagnose service errors.

Service providers and public sources

Depending on configured features, relevant URLs, search terms, images, or extracted text may be sent to third-party services used for domain records, web search, reverse image search, marketplace research, threat checks such as Google Safe Browsing, and optional AI analysis. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

Storage and retention

The consumer checker processes a submission to return an answer and does not intentionally add it to the Evidence Lab recording archive. The Evidence Lab can retain a latest scan and up to 20 local replay records, which may include the original upload, extracted evidence, and results. Active analysis sessions are held in server memory and are limited to 50 sessions. Deployment logs and hosting providers may have separate retention periods.

Your choices and rights

Do not submit sensitive or confidential material. You may request access, correction, or deletion of information that we can reasonably identify as yours. Brand Guard does not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If a browser sends Global Privacy Control, optional analytics and advertising remain off.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit information about a child without appropriate authority.

Terms of use

By using Brand Guard, you agree to use it lawfully and only submit material that you own or have permission to share.

Informational results

Results are based on observable signals available at the time of a check. A "Legit" result means the service did not find material warning signs. It is not a guarantee, endorsement, identity verification, legal conclusion, or financial advice. A seller can change after a check.

Acceptable use

  • Do not upload passwords, full payment-card numbers, government identification, medical records, or private conversations without permission.
  • Do not use the service to harass, surveil, impersonate, or make unlawful decisions about another person.
  • Do not interfere with the service, bypass safeguards, or submit malicious files.

Third-party services

Source links and safer shopping alternatives lead to independent services. Brand Guard does not control their availability, content, prices, or policies. Brand Guard does not process payments.

Availability and changes

This service may change, pause, or stop without notice. These terms may be updated as the product, operator, and legal requirements become final.

Cookie and storage policy

Brand Guard currently does not install analytics or advertising cookies. The homepage uses browser local storage to remember that you reviewed the privacy choices. Local storage is similar to a cookie but stays in your browser until you clear site data.

EssentialOn. Saves the privacy choice.
AnalyticsOff. Not installed.
AdvertisingOff. Not installed.

The preference key is bg_privacy_choice_v1. It records the policy version, disabled optional categories, whether Global Privacy Control was detected, and the time saved. If tracking tools are added later, this policy and the choice interface must be updated before those tools are enabled.

You can reopen Cookie settings from the homepage footer or clear the site's stored data in your browser.

Security

Brand Guard uses layered technical safeguards appropriate to the current prototype, including restrictive browser security headers, file-type inspection, request size limits, protection against server-side requests to private network addresses, capped in-memory sessions, and source-link isolation.

Certification status: Brand Guard does not currently claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, TRUSTe, BBB, or another independent certification. A real badge will appear only after the relevant organization has completed its audit or approval.

Responsible reporting

If you believe you found a security issue, do not access other people's data. Send a concise description and reproduction steps to the contact number below. We will review good-faith reports.

Accessibility

Brand Guard targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA. The site includes keyboard focus states, semantic landmarks, text alternatives for meaningful imagery, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and equivalent text for image-based compositions.

This is a conformance target, not a third-party certification. If any part of the site is difficult to use with assistive technology, contact us with the page, browser, assistive technology, and the task you were trying to complete.
